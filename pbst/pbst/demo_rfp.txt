ZenCloud Cloud Optimization Platform RFP

Customer: ZenCloud

ZenCloud is seeking a cloud optimization solution to reduce rising infrastructure costs.

Requirements:

1. The solution should provide visibility into cloud infrastructure usage and costs.

2. The proposal should explain how cost optimization opportunities will be identified.

3. The solution should include security and access-control considerations.

4. The vendor should describe implementation and onboarding approach.

5. The vendor should describe available customer support.

6. The proposal should clearly state commercial terms.

7. Any unsupported capability, certification, SLA, customer claim, performance result, or integration must be identified for human verification.

8. Commercial terms must follow the terms approved during the commercial review process.
